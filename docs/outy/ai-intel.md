# AI Intel

AI summaries of how the organization is doing, and plain-language questions about team data. **Pro plan.**

## Who can use it
- Owners, admins, team leads and employees can open it.
- Owners, admins and team leads can ask questions.
- Owners and admins can refresh the summary.

## Steps
1. Open **AI Intel** in the sidebar.
2. Read the summary, or type a question and press Ask.
