# Getting started with OutraqHQ

OutraqHQ brings HR (leave, documents, onboarding), work tracking (work logs, tasks, blockers) and performance (increments, fairness, feedback) into one place.

## First day as an employee
1. Open **Work Log** in the sidebar and log what you worked on today.
2. Open **Tasks** to see work assigned to you.
3. Check **Announcements** for company news.
4. Open **Leaves** to see your balance or apply for leave.
5. Update your profile from **Directory → Edit my profile**.

## First day as an owner
1. Invite your team from the **Team** page (linked on your Dashboard, or `/team`) → **Invite** (or import everyone from a spreadsheet — see "inviting-people").
2. Create departments and teams (see "teams-and-departments").
3. Set up leave types under **Leaves → Settings**.
4. Post a welcome announcement.
5. Check **Billing** to see your plan.

Ask Outy "how do I …" at any time for step-by-step help.
