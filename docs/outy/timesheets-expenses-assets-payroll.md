# Timesheets, Expenses, Asset Vault and Payroll

- **Timesheets** — click **Clock in** when you start and **Clock out** when you finish; your hours for the day are recorded.
- **Expenses** — submit a claim with category and amount; approvers approve it from the same page.
- **Asset Vault** — the company's equipment (laptops, monitors, phones) and who it is assigned to.
- **Payroll** — monthly pay records.

All four are in the **Work Telemetry** section of the sidebar.
