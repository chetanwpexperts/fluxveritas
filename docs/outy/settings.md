# Settings

Open **Settings** from the sidebar.

- **Profile** — name, photo and details; change your password.
- **Notifications** — which emails you receive.
- **GitHub** — connect GitHub (see "github-sync").
- **Organization** (owner) — organization name and details.
- **Modules** (owners, admins) — switch off modules you don't use. Paid modules can't be switched on without the plan.
- **Users, Roles, Permissions, Designations, Pending** (owner) — manage people, their roles and pending sign-ups.
