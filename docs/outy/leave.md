# Leave

Apply for leave, check balances and approve requests.

## Apply for leave (everyone)
1. Open **Leaves** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Apply**, pick the leave type, dates (or half day) and a reason.
3. Submit. It shows as **pending** until approved.
4. You can **cancel** a pending request.

## Check your balance
**Leaves** shows each leave type with allocated, used and pending days for this year.

## Approve or reject (approvers)
- Owners, admins and HR can approve or reject any request in the organization.
- Team leads can approve requests from people in the team they lead.
1. Open **Leaves** (HR and admins can use the HR view for everyone's requests).
2. Open the request and **Approve** or **Reject** with a note.

## Set up leave types (owners, admins, HR)
1. Go to **Leaves → Settings**.
2. Add types (for example Casual, Sick, Earned) with days per year, paid or unpaid, carry-forward rules and whether approval is needed.
3. Use **Allocate to all** to give everyone this year's balance.
4. HR can **export** leave data as CSV.
