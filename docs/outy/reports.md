# Reports

**Pro plan.**

- **My Report** — your own work summary: logs, tasks, blockers and trends.
- **Team Reports** — for admins, owners and managers: each person's activity side by side.
- **HR Reports** — for HR, admins and owners: headcount, leave usage and profile completeness.

## Steps
Open the report from the **Reports & Intel** section of the sidebar and choose the period.
