# Increments

Increment recommendations based on monthly contribution scores. **Pro plan.**

## How scores work
- Each month, every person gets a score from 0 to 100 from criteria the owner chooses (tasks completed, task complexity, work log consistency, blocker resolution, attendance, GitHub activity).
- Criteria that don't apply to a role are left out rather than counted as zero.
- Scores are adjusted for approved leave.
- The annual review averages your best 10 months (your 2 lowest months are forgiven) and recommends an increment percentage.

## Employees: see your scores
1. Open **My Increment**.
2. See your monthly scores, the breakdown and your latest review.
3. If you disagree, submit an **appeal** with your reasons.

## Owners: set the policy
1. Go to `/increment/settings` (there is no menu link yet).
2. Set the criteria and their weights, the maximum increment %, the minimum score for an increment and the minimum months of data.
3. Click **Calculate** for a month to score everyone.

## Reviews (owners and admins)
1. Open **Increment Reviews**.
2. Managers add their recommendation and notes.
3. The owner approves the final increment.
