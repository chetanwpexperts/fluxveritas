# Roles

- **Owner** — everything in the organization: people, roles, billing, settings, approvals, increment policy.
- **Admin** — runs day-to-day: projects, team, invites, billing, leave approvals, reports. Can't change roles or organization settings.
- **HR** — directory and HR profiles, leave approvals and leave types, documents, onboarding checklists, HR reports.
- **Team lead** — projects, tasks and sprints, invites, their team's leave approvals and blockers, fairness checks.
- **Employee** — their own work, tasks, leave, blockers, reports and increment.
- **Viewer** — read-only access; can apply for their own leave.

The owner changes someone's role under **Settings → Users**.
