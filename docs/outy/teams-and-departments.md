# Teams and Departments

**Departments** are the big groups (Engineering, Sales, HR). **Teams** sit inside departments and have a team lead.

## Departments (owners and admins)
1. Open **Departments** and click **New department**.
2. Give it a name and type, and optionally a head.
3. Assign people to it from the department page.

## Teams
- Owners and admins create, edit and delete teams.
- Team leads can add and remove members of their team.
1. Open **Teams** and click **New team**.
2. Pick the department and the team lead.
3. Add members from the team page.

A team lead can approve leave for their team, see their team's work logs, and ask Outy "how is my team doing today?".
