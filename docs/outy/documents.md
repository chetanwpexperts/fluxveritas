# Document Center

Company policies, department files and personal documents in one place, with version history.

## Who can use it
- Everyone can view and download the documents shared with them.
- HR, admins and owners can upload, add new versions and delete.

## Upload a document
1. Open **Documents** and click **Upload**.
2. Choose the file, give it a title and category, and choose who can see it.
3. Save. Upload a new version later from the document's page; older versions are kept.
