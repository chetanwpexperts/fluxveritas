# Work Log

A daily record of what you worked on. It feeds team reports, fairness checks and increment scores (log consistency is one of the scoring criteria).

## Who can use it
Everyone. Team leads, admins and owners can also view their team's logs.

## Log today's work
1. Open **Work Log** in the sidebar.
2. Fill in a **category** (for example Development, Client Call, Recruitment), a **title**, how long it took, and optional notes.
3. Optionally link it to one of your tasks — the time is added to that task's actual hours.
4. Save.

## Faster ways to log
- **Quick log**: type a short sentence and it is turned into a log entry.
- **Weekly grid**: fill several days at once.
- **Copy yesterday**: start from yesterday's entry.
- **Templates**: save entries you repeat often and reuse them.

## Team view (team leads, admins, owners)
Open **Work Log → Team** to see who has logged today and who hasn't.

## Tips
- Log every working day; gaps lower your consistency score.
- You can edit or delete your own entries.
