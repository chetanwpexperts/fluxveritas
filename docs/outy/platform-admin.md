# Platform administration (super admins only)

## Organizations
1. Open **Settings → Platform → Organizations**.
2. Search organizations and see owner, plan, subscription, user count and status.
3. Open one for its owner, users, billing and recent account activity.

## Suspend or activate
1. Click **Suspend**, enter a reason (required) and confirm. Members are signed out and can't sign in until it is activated.
2. Click **Activate** to restore access.
Every suspension and activation is recorded in **Audit Logs**.
