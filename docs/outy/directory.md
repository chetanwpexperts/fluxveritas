# Employee Directory

A searchable list of everyone in your organization with their role, department, team and contact details.

## Steps
1. Open **Directory** in the sidebar and search or filter.
2. Click a person to see their profile.
3. Update your own details from **Edit my profile**.

## HR editing
HR, admins and owners can edit anyone's HR details (job title, designation, manager, employment type, probation) from that person's profile.
