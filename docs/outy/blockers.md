# Blockers and Dependencies

Record anything stopping your work — waiting on a person, a vendor, an approval or a meeting — so it gets resolved and is visible to your lead. **Pro plan.**

## Who can use it
- Owners, admins, team leads and employees can report blockers.
- Owners, admins and team leads can resolve and escalate them.

## Report a blocker
1. Open **Blockers** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Report a blocker**.
3. Choose the type (internal person, external vendor, meeting required, waiting for approval, dependency, other).
4. Add a title and description, pick who or what is blocking you, and set a priority.
5. Submit.

## Working a blocker
- The person blocking can **acknowledge** it and add **comments**.
- If you are named as the blocker but it isn't yours, **dispute** the ownership with a reason.
- Leads can **escalate** or **resolve** it with resolution notes.

## Employee status
Leads can mark someone as on leave or away so they aren't flagged for inactivity.

## Why it matters
Blockers open for more than 3 days are raised by the Fairness checks, and blocker resolution is one of the increment criteria.
