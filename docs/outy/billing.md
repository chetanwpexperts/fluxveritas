# Billing and plans

## Plans
- **Free** — ₹0, up to 10 people.
- **Pro** — ₹199 per user per month billed monthly, or ₹149 per user per month billed yearly; minimum 5 users; prices exclude GST.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing; contact sales.

## Who can use it
Owners and admins.

## Upgrade or renew
1. Open **Billing**.
2. Choose monthly or yearly — the page shows users × price, GST and the total.
3. Click **Pay** and complete the payment in Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking).
4. Pro features switch on immediately. Opening a locked feature also offers the same upgrade on the spot.

## Cancel, refund or switch to Free
- Within 7 days of a payment: **Cancel & refund** returns the full amount to the original payment method (usually 5–7 working days).
- After 7 days: **Switch to Free** keeps Pro until the end of the paid period.
- Plans don't renew automatically.

## Receipts
Every payment is listed under **Transaction history** with a printable receipt.
