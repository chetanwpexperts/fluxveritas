# Feedback

## Manager feedback
- Team leads, admins and owners write feedback for people they manage from **Team Feedback**.
- Employees read it under **My Feedback** and can add their own **statement** in response.
- Admins and owners can see all feedback.

## Peer feedback (Pro plan)
1. From an employee's feedback page, a lead or admin clicks **Request peer feedback**.
2. Selected colleagues get a link to a short form (no login needed).
3. Answers are collected and checked for bias; admins and owners can see bias reports.
